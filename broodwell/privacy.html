Privacy Policy

Broodwell — a game for Android by Anand Murali

Effective 16 September 2026 · Last updated 16 September 2026

Broodwell is a single-player game. There is no account, no sign-up and no profile. It shows no ads, contains no analytics and makes no network requests of its own. I do not collect, store or receive any personal information about you, and I operate no servers. Your progress is saved on your own device and stays there.

Your saved game

Your progress — how deep you have reached, your Lore, unlocked nodes, achievements and cosmetics — is written to your device's own app storage and never leaves it. It is not uploaded anywhere, and I cannot see it.

Because it is stored locally, uninstalling Broodwell deletes your progress permanently. There is no cloud backup and no way to restore it.

No advertising

This version of Broodwell shows no advertising of any kind. Nothing in the game requests an ad, and it does not read or transmit your device's advertising identifier. The app requests no advertising permission — you can confirm this yourself in the permission list Google Play shows for the app.

If a future version introduces advertising, that is exactly the kind of change described under Changes below: this page would be updated first, and the release would be declared accordingly on Google Play.

No analytics or tracking

The game contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no advertising SDK in service and no telemetry. It does not profile you, does not build an advertising profile, and does not send your play data anywhere. There is no code in it that contacts a server.

What Google Play collects as the distributor

Installing and updating any app happens through Google Play, which is Google's service and not part of this game. Google collects data about installs, and — if your device is set to send them — crash and performance reports about apps you run. That happens at the Android and Play Store level whether or not an app asks for it.

What reaches me from that is aggregate and anonymous: counts of installs and uninstalls, and anonymised crash reports. None of it names you or lets me contact you. Google's own handling is described in the Google Privacy Policy, and you can change what your device sends in your Android and Google Account settings.

What I receive

Nothing that identifies you, and nothing from inside the game. I do not sell data, share it with advertisers or data brokers, or use it for any purpose. There is no data for me to use.

Children

Broodwell is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 13. I do not knowingly collect information from children — in fact I collect no information from anyone. If you believe a child has provided information through this app, please contact me and I will help have it removed.

Your rights

Because I hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for me to export or erase on request. For anything Google collects as the distributor of the app, use your Google Account privacy controls.

You can remove everything this app stores about you at any time by uninstalling it.

Changes

If the game's data handling changes — advertising, a leaderboard, a cloud save, an analytics service — this page is updated and the "last updated" date at the top changes with it. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
