Privacy Policy

Broodwell — a game for Android by Anand Murali

Effective 15 September 2026 · Last updated 15 September 2026

Broodwell is a single-player game. There is no account, no sign-up, and no profile. I do not collect, store, or receive any personal information about you, and I operate no servers of my own. Your progress is saved on your own device.

The game does use two services provided by Google, and those services collect data of their own. This policy explains exactly what they are, what they collect, and what you can do about it.

Your saved game

Your progress — how deep you have reached, your Lore, unlocked nodes, achievements and cosmetics — is written to your device's own app storage and never leaves it. It is not uploaded anywhere, and I cannot see it.

Because it is stored locally, uninstalling Broodwell deletes your progress permanently. There is no cloud backup and no way to restore it.

Advertising

Broodwell shows ads through Google AdMob. There are two places ads appear: an optional rewarded ad you may choose to watch to double your offline earnings, and an occasional full-screen ad shown when you end a run.

To serve those ads, Google collects information from your device. Google acts as an independent controller of that data — it goes to Google, not to me. Typically it includes:

  • your device's advertising identifier (a resettable ID used for advertising);
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location such as a country or region may be derived;
  • device and app information — model, operating system version, language, and the fact that the ad was shown in Broodwell;
  • interaction with the ad — whether it was viewed, closed, or clicked.

Google's own handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices about ads

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will be asked for your consent before any ad is requested, and you can change that choice later from the game's settings.
  • On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising identifier, and opt out of ad personalisation, in Settings → Privacy → Ads. Deleting it stops personalised advertising; you may still see non-personalised ads.
  • The rewarded ad is entirely optional. Declining it costs you nothing that the game does not give you anyway.

Leaderboard

Broodwell offers an all-time leaderboard through Google Play Games Services. If you are signed in to Play Games on your device, your best score and the player name and ID that Play Games already holds for you may be submitted so the score can appear on the board.

This is optional in the real sense: you can decline or ignore the Play Games sign-in and the entire game still works. Nothing is blocked, nothing is withheld, and your local records are kept either way. Play Games data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

What I receive

Nothing that identifies you. I see only the aggregate, anonymous reports Google provides — for example, how many ads were shown in total, or an anonymised crash report from Google Play. None of it names you or lets me contact you.

I do not sell data, share it with advertisers or data brokers, or use it for any purpose beyond the above.

Children

Broodwell is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 13. I do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through this app, please contact me and I will help have it removed.

Your rights

Because I hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for me to export or erase on request. For the data Google collects, use Google's own controls — the advertising ID settings described above, your Google Account privacy controls, and, in regions where it applies, the consent choice shown inside the game.

You can remove everything this app stores about you at any time by uninstalling it.

Changes

If the game's data handling changes — a new service, a different ad provider — this page is updated and the "last updated" date at the top changes with it. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
