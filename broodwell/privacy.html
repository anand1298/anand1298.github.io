Privacy Policy

Bram's Potion Shop — a game for Android by Anand Murali

Effective 12 October 2026 · Last updated 12 October 2026

Bram's Potion Shop is a single-player game. There is no sign-up and no in-game account. Your progress is saved on your own device. The game shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob, which you choose to watch for a bonus, and it can use Google Play Games for a leaderboard. Those two Google services are the only parts of the game that send data off your device. I do not collect, store or receive personal information about you myself, and I operate no servers.

Your saved game

Your progress (potions found, coins, upgrades, orders, settings and lessons seen) is written to your device's own app storage. It is not uploaded anywhere, and I cannot see it. Uninstalling the game deletes it permanently. There is no cloud save.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers rewarded video ads in four places: drinking a potion for a power, doubling the magic gathered while you were away, getting a new set of orders, and an extra daily card. Every ad is optional: you start it by pressing a button marked with a video icon, and every reward has a way to get it without watching. The game shows no other kind of ad: no banners and no ads that interrupt play.

The ads are served by Google AdMob. To show an ad, measure it, limit fraud and (where you allow it) personalise it, Google's SDK in the app collects and sends to Google:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device and app identifiers;
  • your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it;
  • device information such as model, operating system, language and network type;
  • how you interact with ads (shown, clicked, completed) and app diagnostics such as crash logs from the ad SDK.

Google handles this data under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. The data goes to Google, not to me, and I do not sell it or share it with anyone else.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings ▸ Google ▸ Ads or Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads, depending on your device).

Your consent choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can accept, refuse, or choose which purposes to allow. If you refuse, you may still see non-personalised ads, or no ads, and the game plays the same either way. Players in US states with privacy laws see Google's notice for those states and can opt out of the sale or sharing of their data.

You can change your answer at any time: open Settings in the game and tap Privacy. The button appears wherever the consent form applies to you.

Leaderboard (Google Play Games)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your device, the game signs in to it automatically. It then submits one score, your lifetime Stones, to a leaderboard, and can open Google's leaderboard screen from the game's Best brewers button. Google Play Games shows your Play Games profile name and avatar beside your score to other players. You control that profile, including whether it is visible, in the Google Play Games app and your Google Account. The game does not receive your email address or contacts. If you are not signed in, the game does not sign you in and nothing is submitted. Google's handling of Play Games data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

No analytics of my own

Apart from the two Google services above, the game contains no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no tracking. It does not send your play data anywhere.

What Google Play collects as the distributor

Installing and updating any app happens through Google Play, which is Google's service and not part of this game. Google collects data about installs and, if your device is set to send them, crash and performance reports. What reaches me from that is aggregate and anonymous: counts of installs and anonymised crash reports. None of it names you or lets me contact you.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. Ad requests are not marked as directed at children. I do not knowingly collect information from children. In fact I collect no personal information from anyone myself. If you believe a child has used the app, contact me and I will help.

Your rights

I hold no personal data about you, so I have nothing to export or erase. For the data Google collects through AdMob, Play Games or Google Play, use your Google Account privacy controls, your device's ad settings, and the game's Settings ▸ Privacy button. Uninstalling the game removes everything it stores on your device.

Changes

If the game's data handling changes, this page is updated and the "last updated" date at the top changes with it. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
